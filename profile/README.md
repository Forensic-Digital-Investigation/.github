# Forensic Digital Investigation (FDI)

**FDI** is a private group that connects learners and practitioners in the field of **Digital Forensics & Incident Response (DFIR)**.  
Our primary focus is on building a trusted network and sharing knowledge privately among members.  

Public content is **supplementary** and limited to selected resources, while most educational and outreach material (in Italian) is maintained in the [personal repository](https://github.com/diego-bonatti) of the founder.

---

## Purpose
- Recognize and connect individuals active in the DFIR community.  
- Provide a private space for collaboration and exchange of expertise.  

---

## Public Additions
While FDI is primarily private, the organization may host:  
- **Simulated forensic cases** (selected for public release).  
- **Community-driven resources** that complement private work.  
- **Collaborative projects** open to contributions.
